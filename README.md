# LJQ Theory – Lumière–Jongbae Quantum Contraction Theory

**An alternative cosmological model that explains the accelerated expansion of the universe without dark energy.**

This theory introduces three core mechanisms:

1. **Quantum Contraction of Particles**  
   - All particles shrink gradually over cosmic time due to internal energy consumption.

2. **Temporal Delay Equilibrium Hypothesis (TDEH)**  
   - Asymmetric time flows in gravitational potentials regulate cosmic evolution.

3. **Spacetime Energy Field Hypothesis (SEFH)**  
   - Energy released by particle contraction dynamically reshapes spacetime.

---

## 📘 Highlights

- Resolves the **Hubble tension** without invoking Λ (dark energy)  
- Explains early **galaxy formation** (z > 13)  
- Interprets **CMB uniformity** without inflation  
- Predicts testable **quantum and gravitational phenomena**
